Privacy Policy

MPLH 26 : Block Fall 3D

Developer: MPLH Game · Effective date: October 2, 2026

1. Overview

MPLH Game respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the current release of MPLH 26 : Block Fall 3D handles information when you use the game.

2. Information collected by MPLH Game

The current release does not require an account and does not include developer-operated analytics, advertising, tracking, or cloud-save services. The game does not intentionally collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with MPLH Game.

3. Information stored locally on your device

The game may store gameplay preferences and local progress information on your device, such as best scores, selected game mode, selected speed, music settings, sound-effect settings, and haptics settings. This information remains on the device unless the operating system, the user, or an app uninstall/clear-data action removes it.

4. Sharing features and external services

Sharing is optional and is initiated by the user. On supported platforms, the game may open the operating-system share interface or a web page for a third-party service such as X or Facebook. The game may place score text in the system clipboard when the user chooses a copy/share action. External services have their own privacy policies and may process information according to their own terms. MPLH Game does not control those external services.

5. Internet access

Core gameplay does not require a developer-operated online account or server. Internet access may be used when you choose to open this Privacy Policy, visit the official share page, or open a third-party sharing website.

6. Website hosting

The official Privacy Policy, support page, and share page are hosted using GitHub Pages. When a visitor opens these web pages, GitHub may process technical request information such as the visitor's IP address for security and service operation under GitHub's own privacy practices. MPLH Game does not add analytics or advertising trackers to these pages.

7. Advertising and analytics

The current release does not include advertising SDKs or developer-operated analytics SDKs. If a future release adds analytics, advertising, online accounts, cloud services, or other data-collecting functionality, this Privacy Policy and the relevant store privacy disclosures will be updated before that release is published.

8. Data retention and deletion

Because MPLH Game does not intentionally receive personal information from the current release, MPLH Game does not maintain a server-side user profile to delete. Locally stored game settings and scores can be removed by clearing the app's local data where the platform supports it, or by uninstalling the game.

9. Children's privacy

The game is not designed to collect personal information from children. If the game is later changed in a way that collects personal information, the policy and store disclosures will be updated to reflect the new behavior and applicable legal requirements.

10. Security

The game minimizes privacy risk by keeping gameplay settings and score data local and by not operating a developer account system or remote player database in the current release.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

MPLH Game may update this Privacy Policy when the game, platform requirements, or applicable laws change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published.
